Student FAQs

What you need to know about the School Portal

How Do I Register My Courses?
Once the registration portal is open for the session/semester, go to the "Course Registration" menu, select your programme's available courses for your current level, and submit. You can print your course registration form/slip from the same page once submitted. Registration closes on the deadline set by your department — check with your course adviser if the portal shows registration as closed.
How To Make Payments On The Portal?
Make a transfer to the institution's designated account first. After payment, log in to this portal and check the "Payments/Bills" menu, then select "Make Payment". Enter the required information (amount, reference/teller number) and submit for verification.
How Do I Check My Results?
All your results are listed under the "Exams & Results" menu, broken down by session and semester. You can also view your cumulative GPA/CGPA summary from the same menu. Results can be viewed, printed, or saved as a PDF directly from your device.
How Does The E-Learning Center Work?
The E-Learning Center under the "E-Learning" menu lists every course you're currently registered for. Open a course to access lecture notes, slides, and recorded videos uploaded by your lecturer, join live classes via the Google Classroom or Microsoft Teams links provided (where available), and ask questions or leave comments directly under each material.
How About CBT (Computer-Based Tests)?
The portal uses a third-party CBT tool for course quizzes, tests, and exams. Your lecturer or the exams office will share a link and access details whenever a CBT is scheduled for one of your courses — usually announced under the course's e-learning page or by email/SMS.
How Do I Get / Print My Student ID Card?
Go to "My Profile" → "ID Card" to preview and download your official student ID card as a PDF, or print it directly from the portal. Make sure your profile photo is up to date, since it's what appears on the card — update it under "My Profile" if needed.
How Do I Update My Profile?
You can update basic information (contact details, address, profile photo) from your student portal. Use the "My Profile" menu on the left sidebar and edit the relevant fields.
How Do I Lodge A Complaint Or Get Help?
For registration, payment, or result-related issues, use the "Complaints/Support" menu to raise a ticket — describe the issue clearly and, where relevant, include your matric number and course code. The registry/exams office will respond through the portal or your registered email. For urgent issues, contact your department or the admin office directly.

Lecturer/Staff/Admin FAQs

All You Need To Know

Lecturer's Role
Lecturers can only access and manage the courses assigned to them for the current session/semester (see "My Courses"). From there, you can upload lecture materials and add Google Classroom/Microsoft Teams links under "E-Learning", take attendance by scanning student ID QR codes under "Attendance", and enter/submit scores under "Results".
Course Adviser's Role
A Course Adviser is a lecturer assigned oversight of a specific level/set within a department. In addition to a lecturer's usual access, a course adviser can review their advisees' course registrations, flag irregular registrations before the deadline, and respond to registration-related complaints from their students.
Non-Academic Staff Role
Non-academic staff can log in to the portal if granted access by the administrator, with permissions limited to their assigned unit (e.g. bursary, registry, admissions). This category of staff has restricted privileges on the portal, scoped strictly to their department's tasks.
Admin Role
There are different admin roles as stipulated by the institution (e.g. Registry, Exams & Records, Bursary, ICT). Admin officers manage core academic data — faculties, departments, programmes, course assignments, and session/ semester settings — and can be contacted for help with portal-wide issues.
How Do I Handle Student Complaints?
Complaints submitted through the student "Complaints/Support" menu route to the relevant department/unit based on the category selected. Check "Complaints/Support" → "Assigned To Me" to view, respond to, and mark complaints resolved. Escalate anything outside your unit's scope to the admin office.
