AI Best Practices
  • Always review AI generated content.
  • Specify class level clearly.
  • Specify curriculum requirements.
  • Provide detailed instructions.
  • Always verify examination answers.
  • Review parent communication before sending.
  • Never share confidential information.
Important Security Notice
  • Never enter passwords into AI tools.
  • Never share student PINs.
  • Never share financial credentials.
  • Never upload confidential records to public AI tools.
