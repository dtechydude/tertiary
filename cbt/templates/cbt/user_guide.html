CBT User Guide

Everything you need to know to take an exam or set one up — in one place.

How to Take a CBT Exam

1
Open Your Dashboard

Go to your Exam Dashboard — you'll only see exams for courses you're registered for in the current session and semester.

2
Check the Details Before You Start

Each exam card shows the duration, number of questions, pass mark, and how long the exam stays open. Make sure you have a stable internet connection before clicking Start Examination.

3
Answer the Questions

Questions may be multiple-choice (select one option) or short-answer (type your answer). A countdown timer is always visible at the top — once it hits zero, your exam is submitted automatically.

4
Submit and See Your Score

Click Finish Exam when you're done, or let the timer submit it for you. Your score and pass/fail status appear immediately. You can review your full exam history anytime from "My Results."

Important Rules
  • You can only sit each exam once — there is no retake unless a lecturer/admin clears your previous attempt.
  • The timer cannot be paused. If you lose connection, reopening the exam resumes your original countdown — it does not reset.
  • Exams are only accessible within their configured availability window and daily opening/closing time.

How to Set Up a CBT Exam

1
Quiz Setup (Admin/Staff)

Before questions can be added, an admin or staff member creates the Quiz — linking an Examination, Course, Level, Semester, and the availability window (open/close date and time).

2
Add Questions — One at a Time

From your dashboard, open Add/Manage Questions for any course you're assigned to teach. Fill in the question text, choose Multiple Choice or Short Answer, and mark the correct answer.

3
Or Add Questions in Bulk (CSV)

Setting a full question bank at once is faster with Bulk Upload: download the CSV template, fill in your questions in Excel or Google Sheets, and upload the file — the system validates every row and shows you exactly what was saved and what needs fixing before it commits anything.

4
Review Student Results

Use View Student Scores to filter results by Examination or Level for your assigned courses, and export them to CSV for your records or the registrar.

Important Rules
  • You can only manage quizzes and question banks for courses you are formally assigned to (per your Course Assignment record).
  • Only one quiz is allowed per Course, per Examination, per Semester and Session — this prevents accidental duplicates.
  • In your CSV upload, question_type must be MCQ or SHORT, and for MCQ, correct_answer must be A, B, C, or D.
